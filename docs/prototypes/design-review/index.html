Try These

  • Click Add a photo and see whether the Today screen feels complete quickly.
  • Type one or two nice things and decide if the ritual feels lightweight.
  • Add a tiny detail like a funny phrase, favorite snack, or personal milestone.
  • Switch between Today, Memories, Calendar, Insights, and Settings.
  • Tag people on Today and filter Memories by each kid.
  • Click calendar days and check whether indicators are understandable.
  • Judge whether the warm palette feels premium or too soft.
